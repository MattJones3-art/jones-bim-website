SHEET F-01 / FAQ

Common questions

What do you need from us?

Drawings and specs (PDF or CAD) and any models that already exist. We confirm scope and file formats before we price anything.

What software do you use?

Revit for modeling, Navisworks and Revizto for coordination, and Civil 3D and Bluebeam for as-builts and markups.

How do you price your work?

A fixed fee, broken into phases: discovery, modeling by discipline, and coordination rounds. The price of an extra coordination round is stated in the proposal.

Can you coordinate models from different teams?

Yes. We bring models from multiple consultants and trades together and report the conflicts.

How long does it take?

It depends on size, disciplines, and the condition of the design files. We give a schedule in the proposal.

SHEET H-01 / OUR WORK

Our work

Real problems found by checking models and the field against the drawings. Client and project names are withheld.

CASE 01

As-built verification: substation column

PROBLEM
An as-built survey showed every anchor bolt in the set cast the same distance off.
WHAT WE DID
Traced the error to a single incorrect centerline dimension repeated across the set, notified the engineers, and had the drawings updated to match the field.
RESULT
No rework and no schedule hit. Found on paper it costs hours; found at steel erection it costs weeks.
CASE 02

Clash resolution: sprinkler pipe and steel beam

PROBLEM
A fire sprinkler pipe was clashing with a structural steel beam.
WHAT WE DID
Remodeled the pipe to run below the beam.
RESULT
Clash resolved in the model, with the new routing shown to the team.
CASE 03

Shop drawing: anchor bolt template

PROBLEM
A catenary mast foundation needed a template to hold its anchor bolts in position.
WHAT WE DID
Detailed a 3 ft 4 in template with a 1 ft center opening for placing concrete, slotted holes to secure it to the formwork, and a size 2 in wider than the foundation so it rests on the formwork.
RESULT
A fabrication-ready drawing built around how the work is placed in the field.
